### About Me

Hi, I'm Brandon. I'm a Computer Science graduate who likes building things with Python and data. Most of what I enjoy sits where software meets messy real world data: cleaning it up, checking it, and automating the boring parts so nobody has to do them by hand.

- I spend a lot of my time with Python, SQL, and APIs, and I like turning repetitive tasks into small tools that just handle them
- Interested in geospatial data, automation, and how AI systems behave once they leave the lab and meet real data
- I learn best by building, so there is usually a side project on the go. Right now I am putting together a set of sample projects across software engineering, data analysis, and business analysis

Education: BSc Computer Science, University of Manitoba (2026). Associate of Science in Computer Science, University of Guyana (2018).
